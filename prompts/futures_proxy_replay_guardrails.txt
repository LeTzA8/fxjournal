APPROXIMATE REPLAY GUARDRAILS
==============================

These rules apply when a trade includes replay_accuracy: "approximate".

In approximate replay mode, chart context uses CFD bars from the trader's
MT5 broker as a visual proxy for a futures trade. The CFD bars and the
futures execution are from different instruments and price levels. The
imported futures execution data (fill prices, timestamps, PnL) is always
the source of truth.

This file is NOT wired into existing weekly review prompts (Phase 1 scaffold).
It defines the contract for Phase 2, when proxy bars may be used in AI review.
Do not add these rules to dashboard_advice.txt, dashboard_advice_rewrite.txt,
weekly_review_followup.txt, or journal_chat.txt until Phase 2 is implemented.


SOURCE OF TRUTH
---------------
The following fields from the imported futures CSV are exact and reliable:
- original_symbol / contract_code   (e.g. NQ, NQH6)
- entry_time / exit_time            (imported, converted to UTC)
- entry_price / exit_price          (actual CME fill prices)
- quantity / contracts
- pnl / fees

The following data comes from a CFD proxy and is approximate:
- chart bars (source: mt5_cfd, e.g. NAS100)
- any bar-derived measurements (MFE, MAE, bar shapes, tick volume)


TIMING AND STRUCTURE — ALLOWED
-------------------------------
Use timing and structure language freely:
- "during the New York open"
- "around the time of the entry"
- "in the context of a pullback"
- "near a session overlap"
- "the timing suggests the entry was during a continuation leg"
- "the proxy chart indicates this was a choppy period"
- "the trade was held across two sessions"


EXACT PRICE CLAIMS — NOT ALLOWED
---------------------------------
Do NOT make exact price claims when the only evidence is proxy CFD bars:
- Do NOT say: "you entered exactly at the low"
- Do NOT say: "your stop was 3 points below structure"
- Do NOT say: "price missed your TP by 0.25"
- Do NOT say: "MFE was exactly 2.1R"
- Do NOT say: "a liquidity sweep confirmed your exact futures price"
- Do NOT say: "you should have held to this specific price level"
- Do NOT cite mfe_r, mae_r, tp_capture_pct, or exact TP/SL reach
  as evidence when replay_accuracy is approximate


BAR-DERIVED METRICS — NOT AVAILABLE
------------------------------------
The following fields are suppressed or unreliable for proxy trades:
- mfe_r, mae_r
- tp_capture_pct, closed_before_tp, closed_before_sl
- post_exit_direction, post_exit_tp_reached
- exact entry bar body/wick/volume microstructure

If these fields are absent from the payload, do not attempt to infer them
from the CFD proxy bars or estimate them from the execution prices alone.


COACHING APPROACH FOR PROXY TRADES
-----------------------------------
Focus coaching on:
- Session context and time-of-day patterns
- Hold duration vs the trader's typical duration
- Broad structural context (was price in an impulsive or corrective phase
  at the approximate entry time, based on the proxy chart)
- Behavioral signals: post-loss sequences, risk changes, re-entry patterns
  (these derive from execution data and are fully reliable)

If a coaching point would require exact bar-derived price evidence that
is unavailable due to approximate replay, omit the price claim or note
that exact price context is not available for this trade. Do not manufacture
precision from approximate data.


DISCLAIMER — INCLUDE WHEN CITING PROXY TRADES
----------------------------------------------
When referencing chart context for a proxy trade, include a brief note such as:
"This is based on approximate chart context from a CFD proxy — exact price
levels are not confirmed."
