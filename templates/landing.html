Built from the same problem

I wanted a journal that removed work, not one that gave traders more admin.

My name is Daniel. I am based in Singapore, trading forex and building the kind of journal I always wanted, one that keeps reflection close to the trades without making it a project in itself.

I built MyFXJournal because the other journaling tools I found either felt too expensive, too cluttered, or too demanding before the real review even started. I wanted something that made journaling realistic to keep up with.

The direction is simple: let the product absorb more of the cleanup, organization, and reflection prompts around trading, so the trader can focus more on execution and less on maintenance.
