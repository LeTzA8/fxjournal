Common questions

How does the free trial work?

Your core journal stays free. The 14-day trial covers premium workflow features such as MT5 sync, weekly-review follow-up chat, and advanced replay access where available. No credit card required.

What happens when paid tiers launch?

Free tier stays free. Advanced workflow features will move behind Trader or Pro access when billing opens. Until then, upgrade actions are waitlist and intent only.

Why join the waitlist now?

Waitlist members hear first about early-access rates before public pricing goes live. You're also helping shape what gets built next — we reach out to waitlist members when new features roll out.

What is Advanced Replay?

Standard Replay shows your trade on a 5m or 15m chart. Advanced Replay covers lower timeframes such as 1m and extended replay history when those surfaces are available.

What does MT5 sync cost?

MT5 sync is part of the same 14-day premium workflow trial. After the trial, your journal and existing trade history remain available; sync reactivation is waitlist-first until billing opens.

Can I cancel anytime?

Monthly and annual options are planned, but final billing terms will be published before subscriptions open. There is no paid billing active yet.
