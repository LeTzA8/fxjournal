Common questions

Is it really free right now?

Yes. The product is in open beta and currently live features are free. No credit card required. When paid tiers launch, existing users will hear first about early access pricing.

What happens when beta ends?

Free tier stays free. Trader and Pro features will require a subscription. Everyone who signed up during beta will hear first and get a grandfathered discount as a thank-you for being early.

Why join the waitlist now?

Waitlist members hear first about early-access rates before public pricing goes live. You're also helping shape what gets built next — we reach out to waitlist members when new features roll out.

What is Advanced Replay?

Standard Replay shows your trade on a 5m or 15m chart. Advanced Replay is the planned Trader rollout for lower timeframes such as 1m and extended replay history.

What does MT5 sync cost?

MT5 sync is free during beta. When billing launches, it is included in Trader and Pro. Free accounts will have a 14-day trial before needing to upgrade.

Can I cancel anytime?

Monthly and annual options are planned, but final billing terms will be published before subscriptions open. There is no active paid billing during beta.
